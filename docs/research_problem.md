# Research Problem

## Motivation

During disaster response, locating survivors quickly is critical.
UAVs can cover large areas faster than ground teams, but limited
battery, uncertain survivor locations, obstacles, and changing
environmental conditions make efficient search difficult.

## Problem

Conventional UAV search systems may rely on predefined or fixed
search patterns. Such approaches do not necessarily adapt to
information obtained during the mission.

ARES investigates whether an adaptive search strategy can improve
survivor search efficiency under limited time and energy.

## Research Question

Can an adaptive, risk-aware and energy-aware UAV search strategy
improve survivor discovery efficiency compared with conventional
search strategies?

## Baselines

- Random search
- Grid/lawn-mower search
- Nearest-target search

## Proposed Approach

ARES dynamically updates its search strategy based on:

- Survivor probability
- Detection confidence
- Search uncertainty
- Survivor priority
- Distance
- Estimated energy cost

## Evaluation Metrics

- Survivors detected
- Time to first survivor
- Time to locate all survivors
- Search coverage
- Flight distance
- Energy consumption
- Localization accuracy
